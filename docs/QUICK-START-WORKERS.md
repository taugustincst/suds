# SUDS quick start for workers

One card per task, one screen each, in the words the app uses on its buttons. For navigators, peer navigators,
outreach workers, clinicians and supervisors. Print the cards you need. The full guide is [USER_GUIDE.md](USER_GUIDE.md).

Three things work everywhere:

* **+ Log** (the blue button; bottom right on a phone) records anything: a visit, a street outreach contact, a call,
  a text, a note, a to-do, a referral or time.
* **Home** shows **To-dos for today** and where you left off.
* Every page has a **?** that explains it. Nothing you do deletes a record for good, so you cannot break anything.

Your menu is built for your role, so you may not see every card's button. If one is missing, ask your supervisor.

---

## 1. Log a contact with a client

1. Open the client (type their last name, phone or code in the search box), or start from **+ Log**.
2. Choose **Log a visit**, **Phone call** or **Text message**.
3. Fill in what you did and when. Keep the short summary free of names and health details.
4. Need to check back? Set **Remind me to follow up on** (a visit) or **Remind me to call back on** (a call). The
   to-do appears on your list for that day.
5. Paid time? Tick **Also log this as a time entry** (a call: **Also log as time entry**).
6. Press **Save** (a visit), **Log call** (a call) or **Log text** (a text).

You closed the form by accident? Open **Log a visit** again and choose *Resume* when it asks **Resume your unsent
visit?**

---

## 2. Street outreach (no names), with or without signal

1. **+ Log → Street outreach contact**, or **Street outreach** in the menu.
2. Pick the **Contact** type and **Where** (roughly, never an address).
3. Count the **Supplies given** with − and +. Handing out the same bundle again? Tap **↻ Same as last contact**.
4. **Participant code (optional)**: only if the person gives one. **Notes (optional)**: what happened, never who.
5. Press **Save contact**. Wrong count? Press **Undo** in the message within 10 seconds.

**No signal?** Press **Save contact** anyway. A contact with no participant code and no notes is kept on the phone
(*No signal: the contact … is kept on this phone*) and sent by itself when you are back online. If it has a code or
notes, choose **Keep it without the code and notes**. You will see the waiting contacts under **Waiting to send**;
**Send now** tries again. They stay on the phone for you if you sign out, and go the next time you sign in.

**My shift** under the form counts what you logged today.

---

## 3. Follow-ups and to-dos

1. **To-dos** in the menu (or **To-dos for today** on Home) lists what is due. **Overdue** ones are marked.
2. Done? Tick its box in the list. Several at once: tick them in the **Select** column, then **Mark selected done**.
   On Home, the box marks it done and the message offers **Undo**; tapping the to-do's title opens it (or the call,
   visit or referral it came from). On a phone's To-dos list, **Open the call** (or visit, or referral) is on the row.
3. New reminder: **+ Log → To-do** (or **+ Add a to-do** on the To-dos page). Use the quick dates under the due date:
   **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **In 3 days**, **In a week**.
4. Push one back: open it and press **Tomorrow**.

A follow-up date on a visit, call or referral makes its own to-do. Change the date and the to-do moves; clear it and
the to-do is cancelled.

---

## 4. Write and sign a note

1. **+ Log → Note**, or **+ New note** on the Notes page, or **Add a note** inside **Log a visit**.
2. Choose the **Note type** and write (a clinician's starts as a **Clinical** note in the **Progress** format). It
   saves as a draft while you type.
3. Finished? Press **Save & sign**, or open the draft later and press **Sign & lock**, then **Sign note** in the
   *Electronic signature* box. A signed note cannot be changed.
4. Want your supervisor to look at it? Tick **Request supervisor co-sign / review** before signing.

Drafts you have not signed are under **Unfinished notes** on Home. Sign them the same day if you can.

---

## 5. Make a referral, and record what happened

1. **+ Log → Make a referral**, or **+ Make a referral** on the client's record.
2. Choose the provider and the reason. If SUDS says there is no consent on file, record the client's consent first;
   the referral waits for you.
3. Set **Follow-up due**: a to-do reminds you to find out what happened.
4. When you know: open the referral and press **Record outcome**. Choose **What happened**; if it did not happen,
   **If it did not happen, why**; add **Outcome in your words**. Press **Record outcome**. Its to-do closes.

---

## 6. My phone as a field device

You need this only to see your clients and to-dos with no signal. Street outreach contacts already wait on the
phone (card 2).

1. Open **Set up this phone for the field** (at the foot of **Street outreach**, or in the *Offline* banner).
2. Either set it up yourself: **Open the offline copy of SUDS**, then **Keep only what I need in the field** and
   **Sync now**; or press **Ask my administrator**. The page shows *Asked*, *Approved* or *Not approved*.
3. A field device keeps only your own recent clients (name, participant code, safety flags), your contacts, to-dos,
   supplies and lists. Notes, documents and consents stay at the office.
4. Sync whenever you have signal. The top of the page says what is still to send (**⇅ 3 to send**).

---

## 7. For supervisors: your team at a glance

1. **Supervision** in the menu.
2. **Notes waiting for your countersignature** and **Unsigned notes across your team**: the notes that need you, and
   the drafts your team has not signed yet.
3. **Waiting to hear what happened**: referrals with no outcome yet. **Remind worker** puts a to-do on their list;
   **Record outcome** records it yourself.
4. **Staff time waiting for approval**: approve hours from the time your team logged (**My time**).
5. **Referrals needing attention** and **Your clients who need a check-in** show what is slipping.
