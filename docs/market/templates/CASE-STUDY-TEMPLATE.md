# Case study template (CBO references)

For the first two or three community-based organisations that pilot SUDS. Publish only with written permission
from the organisation (the pilot evaluation records it, [PILOT-KIT.md](../PILOT-KIT.md), section 7). Never
include client information, client stories that could identify someone, or screenshots with real records — use
the sample data set for pictures. Numbers must come from the pilot's measured metrics, with baselines, and be
approved by the organisation.
