Accessibility statement

SUDS — SUD Navigator Services Tracker

Everyone who records services, referrals and follow-ups in SUDS should be able to use it — including staff who use a screen reader, magnification, voice control, a keyboard instead of a mouse, dark or high-contrast settings, or large text on a phone.

Conformance status

SUDS aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA, the standard adopted for state and local government web content under Title II of the Americans with Disabilities Act. Per the DOJ Title II rule as extended, public entities with a population of 50,000 or more must meet it by April 26, 2027, and smaller public entities and special district governments by April 26, 2028 (confirm the current dates with your county’s ADA coordinator or ada.gov). SUDS is partially conformant: it meets the level A and AA criteria apart from the limitations listed below. A criterion-by-criterion Accessibility Conformance Report (VPAT® 2.5, WCAG edition) is available from your SUDS administrator.

What SUDS does

  • Every page, client-record section, Settings section and everyday dialog is checked automatically on every release against all WCAG 2.0 and 2.1 A and AA rules, for each staff role, in light and dark themes, on desktop and phone screens, and at 200% text.
  • Creating a client, logging a visit, recording a consent and a referral that relies on it, and writing and signing a note are tested with the keyboard alone.
  • Each page has a “Skip to content” link, its own title, and headings in order. Dialogs keep the keyboard inside them, close with Escape and return you to where you were.
  • Colour is never the only signal: overdue and at-risk values also show ⚠ and words, and links in text are underlined.
  • Text can be enlarged to 200%, and SUDS works on a 320-pixel-wide screen without scrolling sideways.
  • Before signing you out after inactivity, SUDS warns you a minute ahead and offers Stay signed in.

Known limitations

  • The maximum session length your administrator sets (12 hours unless changed) ends a session without a warning. The inactivity sign-out does warn and can be extended, and what you had typed into a form is kept for after you sign in again.
  • Pictures without a caption are described only as “Picture 2 of 5”, and uploaded documents (scanned consents, signed forms, policies) are only as accessible as the file. Add a caption to each picture, and upload tagged PDFs where you can.
  • PDFs SUDS produces (a consent printed for signing, county forms) are not tagged, so a screen reader gets no headings or field labels in them; the same information is on the client’s Consents tab and the Forms page. Excel exports have header rows but have not been assessed on their own.
  • Date fields use your browser’s own calendar; a date can always be typed instead.
  • Automated checks run in Chromium; testing with each screen reader is done by hand before releases.

Get help or report a barrier

  1. Tell your programme first. Your SUDS administrator can change settings and accounts straight away. Programme contact: .
  2. Report it to the SUDS project at github.com/taugustincst/suds/issues, titled “Accessibility:”, saying which page, what you were trying to do, and your browser and assistive technology. Never include client information. Your administrator can file it for you.

We aim to acknowledge an accessibility report within 2 business days and to provide a fix or workaround within 10 business days. If you are not satisfied, contact your county’s ADA coordinator.
