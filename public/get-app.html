Use SUDS on your phone or tablet

There is no app to install. SUDS is used in the browser at the office address, on any phone, tablet or computer. Everything you do on one device is on every other device right away, because they all talk to the same office SUDS.

There is no app to install. SUDS on this device runs entirely inside your browser, with no office server behind it, so add it to your home screen and it opens like an app — with no connection at all.

1. Open SUDS

On the office Wi-Fi (or a connection IT gave you), open this address in your browser and sign in with your own account.

Open this address in your browser and choose Sign up to create your account on the device (or Log in if you already have one there).

2. Add it to the home screen

iPhone / iPad: in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
Android, Chrome or Edge on a computer: open the browser menu (⋮) and choose Install app (or Add to Home screen).

SUDS then opens like an app, with the same sign-in, sign-out and rules as at the office. Installing from this page installs SUDS itself, not these instructions.

3. Stay connected

SUDS needs a connection to the office server. Without one you can look at what the browser has cached, but nothing can be saved until you reconnect — the page tells you when that happens.

Remove the certificate warning (optional)

Your records stay on this device. Everything you enter is stored encrypted in this browser on this device; nothing is sent anywhere and it does not sync with an office server. Clearing the browser's site data, or losing the device, erases it — download a backup regularly from the This device page. Open SUDS.
